About Amba Village

Amba Village is located in Shahuwadi Taluka of Kolhapur District and is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Maharashtra. Surrounded by the Sahyadri mountain ranges, dense forests and breathtaking valleys, Amba offers a peaceful escape from city life.

Natural Heritage

Amba is known for its rich biodiversity, scenic beauty and pleasant climate throughout the year. The village is surrounded by green forests, hills and wildlife that attract nature lovers from all over the country.

Its unique location in the Sahyadri mountain range makes it a perfect destination for eco-tourism and adventure tourism.
